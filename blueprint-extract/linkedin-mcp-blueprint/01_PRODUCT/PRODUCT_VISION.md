# Product Vision

Build a secure, consent-driven MCP server that exposes LinkedIn API capabilities to AI clients such as Claude and ChatGPT, subject to LinkedIn authorization and client-side MCP support. The long-term product may be monetized as a subscription service.

The product is an integration bridge—not a scraper, account automation bot, or workaround for LinkedIn restrictions. Value should come from permitted data retrieval, account-context-aware actions, transparent confirmations, and user-controlled workflows.
